New Patient Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept my dental insurance, and what if I don't have any?

We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans and will gladly file all claims on your behalf to maximize your benefits. For patients without insurance, we offer an affordable in-house Dental Wellness Plan that covers routine cleanings and provides steep discounts on all cosmetic and restorative procedures.

What cosmetic and restorative procedures do you perform in-office?

Unlike other practices that refer you out to expensive specialists, our team handles routine family cleanings, porcelain veneers, professional teeth whitening, clear aligners, and complete dental implant surgeries right here under one roof in Mesquite.

I experience severe dental anxiety. How does your office handle this?

Dental anxiety is incredibly common, and our office is designed to be a judgment-free, comfortable environment. We offer gentle care techniques, noise-canceling headphones, warm blankets, and safe sedation options (like nitrous oxide) to ensure you remain completely relaxed during your visit.

How quickly can I get an appointment, and do you handle emergencies?

New patient routine exams can typically be scheduled within the same week. If you are experiencing a severe toothache or a dental emergency, call us immediately—we reserve dedicated emergency time blocks every single day to provide same-day pain relief for both new and existing patients.
