Frequently Asked Emergency Questions

What should I do if my tooth gets completely knocked out?

Handle the tooth strictly by the crown, not the delicate root. Gently rinse with water if dirty, attempt to re-seat it, or submerge it in milk and call us immediately.

Does Meridian Dental offer same-day emergency appointments?

Yes, we reserve morning and afternoon blocks specifically for urgent local walk-ins and phone calls.
