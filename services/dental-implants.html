The Dental Implant Journey at Meridian

From high-definition 3D diagnostics to final crown placement, your restoration is engineered with clinical precision:

1. 3D Cone Beam CT Imaging: We capture a 3D digital map of your jawbone density, nerves, and sinus anatomy to plan exact post placement.

2. Precision Post Placement: A biocompatible titanium post is gently set into the jawbone, where it naturally integrates over 3–6 months.

3. Custom Ceramic Crown Restoration: We design and fit a custom ceramic crown matching the exact shade and contours of your natural bite.

Frequently Asked Dental Implant Questions

How long do dental implants typically last?

Dental implants are designed as a permanent tooth replacement solution. With good oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, the titanium implant post can last 25 years to a lifetime.

Am I a candidate for dental implants if I have bone loss?

Yes! Meridian Dental provides preparatory bone grafting and sinus lifts to regenerate jawbone volume, making dental implants possible even after long-term tooth loss.

What is the recovery time after a dental implant placement?

Most patients return to work within 24 to 48 hours with minimal discomfort. Complete osseointegration (bone fusion) generally takes 3 to 6 months before attaching your final permanent ceramic crown.
