Frequently Asked Cosmetic Questions

How long do porcelain veneers typically last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental cleanings at Meridian Dental, high-quality custom porcelain veneers typically last between 10 to 15 years or longer.

Is professional in-office teeth whitening safe for sensitive teeth?

Yes! Our clinical in-office whitening treatments include integrated desensitizing agents formulated to lift stains up to 8 shades while protecting tooth enamel.

What is the difference between dental bonding and porcelain veneers?

Composite bonding uses a tooth-colored resin sculpted directly onto minor chips in a single visit, while porcelain veneers are custom ceramic shells designed for full smile makeovers.
