Frequently Asked Family Dentistry Questions

How often should my family visit the dentist for cleanings?

The American Dental Association recommends visiting your dentist every six months for a comprehensive exam, professional dental hygiene cleaning, and routine oral cancer screenings.

At what age should children have their first dental exam?

We recommend bringing your child in when their first tooth erupts or by their first birthday to establish positive oral habits and track jaw growth early.

What is the difference between a regular cleaning and a deep cleaning?

A standard prophylaxis cleaning removes plaque and tartar above the gumline, whereas periodontal deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) cleans beneath the gums to treat periodontal disease.
